I’m a 3x founder, software engineer, and fractional CTO. I design systems, lead technical teams, and help companies make difficult technology decisions.

I started programming at age 13 when I needed to add some functionality to my [PHP-Nuke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PHP-Nuke) website. I quickly advanced to reverse engineering x86 binaries and creating game hacks. In high school I famously reversed-engineered Warcraft III's battle.net authentication protocol based on [SRP](http://srp.stanford.edu). The remnants of my code still exist in [BNCSUtil](https://github.com/BNETDocs/bncsutil). I then grabbed a degree in Electrical Engineering from [Georgia Tech](https://www.gatech.edu) before starting my career in machine learning and data science.

Since then, I’ve worked on recommendation engines, financial messaging systems, consumer apps, blockchains, and enterprise software. I’ve founded companies and led engineering teams, while continuing to write code and design systems.

Currently I'm most interested in how to incorporate AI agents into existing systems and business workflows.

### Career highlights

- In 2011, my team placed ninth in the [Yahoo! KDD Cup](https://www.kdd.org/kdd2011/kddcup_old.shtml) using a graph-based “square counting” method for recommendation.
- Led the engineering team at [GetGlue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tvtag) that developed a social guide for TV with 4 million users. GetGlue was eventually acquired by i.TV.
- Worked on a skunkworks team for [Thomson Reuters](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/) that successfully reversed engineered and replaced Microsoft's Live Communication Server to use in Eikon. We used a new language at the time called [Go](https://go.dev/).
- Helped [SonicCloud](https://www.soniccloud.com/) launch the first version of their hearing aid app.
- Helped [Loom Network](https://loomx.io/) launch their DPoS sidechain for Ethereum.
- Cofounded [OnFrontiers](https://www.onfrontiers.com) in 2016 becoming its CTO. I led the development of its flagship product called Access which is running today.
