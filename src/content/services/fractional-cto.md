## How I can help

### Technology direction

Turn business priorities into a practical technical roadmap. Evaluate architecture, build-versus-buy decisions, and the tradeoffs involved in modernizing an existing product.

### Engineering leadership

Help your team establish clear ownership, improve delivery, and hire well. Work with founders and engineering teams to align technical decisions with the needs of the business.

### Systems and operating costs

Review infrastructure, reliability, and security priorities. Find opportunities to simplify systems, reduce recurring costs, and bring AI into workflows where it solves a real problem.

## Selected experience

### OnFrontiers · CTO & AI Lead

Led a 10-person engineering team across architecture, infrastructure, security, hiring, and delivery. Restructured engineering and product organizations, reducing annual operating costs by approximately $2M, and led infrastructure and vendor optimization that saved an additional $200K annually.

### OnFrontiers · CTO & Co-Founder

Led the transition from a Ruby on Rails MVP to a production platform in Go and React, implementing much of the initial system and hiring the engineering team. The company used that platform to reach more than $1M in revenue and raise $2M in funding.

### GetGlue · VP of Engineering and Data Science

Managed 14 engineers, with responsibility for hiring, performance reviews, technical decisions, and day-to-day operations. Scaled the backend to support 4 million users and more than 10,000 requests per second.
